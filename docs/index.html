Bubblecast

Record your screen with your camera in a bubble. Everything stays on your Mac.

Download for macOS Source on GitHub

Screen + camera bubble

Record any display with your camera in a circle, rounded square or 4:3 bubble, in three sizes.

Full-screen camera

Switch between the bubble and a full-screen camera with one click, even while recording.

Private by design

Videos are saved as MP4 files on your Mac. No account, no upload unless you choose it.

Small files, clear voice

Efficient HEVC video and an automatic voice level that keeps room noise down.

Library

Watch, rename, search and tidy every recording, with its size, length and cloud status.

Google Drive, optional

Connect once to upload a video, share a link, stop sharing or update it. Bubblecast only sees the files it uploads.

Install

  1. Download Bubblecast-<version>.pkg from the latest release.
  2. Open it and follow the installer. Bubblecast opens by itself.
  3. Allow the camera, microphone and screen recording in the setup window.

Requires macOS 15 Sequoia or later.
