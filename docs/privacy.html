Bubblecast Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Bubblecast is a free, open-source screen recorder for macOS, made by Mehedi Hasan Sarkar. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: your recordings stay on your Mac, and nothing is sent anywhere unless you connect Google Drive and choose to upload.

What stays on your Mac

  • Screen, camera and microphone recordings are saved as MP4 files in a folder on your Mac (by default ~/Movies/Bubblecast, or a folder you choose).
  • App settings (bubble size, shape, filter, position, chosen devices) are stored in macOS preferences on your Mac.
  • Bubblecast has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own. The developer receives no data from the app.

Camera, microphone and screen recording

macOS asks for your permission before Bubblecast can use the camera, microphone or screen recording. They are used only to show your camera bubble and to create the recordings you start. You can turn these permissions off at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Google Drive (optional)

If you click Connect Google Drive, Google asks you to allow access. Bubblecast requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files it creates in your Drive. It cannot read any other file in your Google Drive.

  • What is sent to Google: only the recordings you choose to upload (or new recordings, if you turn on automatic upload), their file names, and sharing changes you make (create or remove a share link, rename, update or delete a file).
  • What Bubblecast stores: the Google sign-in token is kept in your macOS Keychain on your Mac. The IDs and share links of uploaded files are kept in a file in your Mac's Application Support folder.
  • Sharing: a file is shared only when you choose "Share" or "Copy link". Anyone with that link can view the video until you choose "Stop sharing" or remove it from Drive.
  • Disconnecting: "Disconnect Google Drive" removes the sign-in from your Mac and asks Google to revoke it. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already uploaded stay in your Drive until you delete them.

Data from Google APIs is used only to provide these features, inside the app on your Mac. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone, and not read by any person other than you. Bubblecast's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Bubblecast is a general tool and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. The full history is in the public source code.

Contact

Questions or requests: please open an issue at github.com/mehedisarkar2k/bubblecast/issues.
